Data Management

How to use

  1. Save your pilgrim details in the Pilgrims tab
  2. On TTD website, log in and select your booking date & slot
  3. Select the number of pilgrims and click Continue
  4. When the pilgrim details form appears, click Start Auto-Fill in this extension
  5. The extension will fill all fields, click Continue, then click Pay Now automatically

Tips

  • Fill pilgrims in order (1, 2, 3…) — the extension fills from first saved pilgrim
  • If you book for fewer people than saved, the extra entries are ignored
  • Name must match your Aadhaar Card exactly
  • Works for both SED and Special Sevas darshan booking
